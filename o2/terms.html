Purpose and health disclaimer

O2 provides guided breathing exercises, breath-hold tests, CO₂ and O₂ tolerance tables, and training plans for education, fitness and well-being. O2 is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent medical conditions. It does not provide medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting a breath-hold training program.

Breath-hold training can cause dizziness, hypoxia and loss of consciousness. Follow every in-app safety instruction. Practice in a safe, dry setting; never use the App for breath-holds in water, while driving or while operating machinery. Stop immediately if you feel unwell. Obtain medical clearance if you are pregnant or have cardiovascular, respiratory or other relevant health concerns. These exercises are not a substitute for qualified freediving instruction or supervision.

O2 — Privacy Policy

This policy applies to O2 Breath Hold Trainer on Android and iOS. TrueBloom Ltd is responsible for the App. Contact support@truebloom.ltd for privacy questions or requests.

Information stored on your device

O2 does not require an account. Your personal-best breath-hold times, training history and statistics, feedback, safety acknowledgements, health-screening selections, settings and reminder preferences are stored locally. Android also stores local purchase-entitlement and activation state. We do not upload these training or health records to our own servers or send them to analytics services. The App does not display advertising.

Android installation analytics

Starting with Android version 1.0.3, O2 uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure first opens and basic app usage. The SDK automatically collects an app-instance identifier, app and device information, first-open and session events, approximate location derived from IP addresses, and in-app purchase events when available. It does not receive your breath-hold results, training history, health-screening answers, Health Connect records or activation codes. We do not set an account-based analytics user ID. Advertising ID collection, advertising storage, advertising user data, personalized advertising signals and automatic screen reporting are disabled in the App.

Analytics data is sent to Google over encrypted connections and processed to provide aggregate usage reports. This Android integration does not add analytics to the iOS App. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information. Clearing Android App data or uninstalling the App removes the local analytics identifier; previously collected data remains subject to the Analytics service’s retention settings. You can contact support@truebloom.ltd about privacy requests. Because O2 has no user accounts and we do not associate analytics identifiers with your identity, we may be unable to identify your individual analytics records.

Health Connect and Apple Health

Health integration is optional and requires your permission. On Android, O2 requests permission to write mindfulness sessions to Health Connect. A record contains the completed session’s start and end time and training-plan name. O2 does not request permission to read your Health Connect records. On iOS, permitted completed workouts may be saved to Apple Health. Health data is used only for the requested fitness and well-being integration, and not for advertising, profiling, data brokerage or sale.

You control these integrations in O2 Settings and the platform’s health settings. You can revoke permissions at any time. Health Connect and Apple Health manage which other authorized apps can access their records; O2 does not automatically send those records to other apps or to our servers. Records already written to a health platform may remain after O2 is uninstalled; delete them through that platform’s data controls.

Platform services and purchases

Google Play or Apple processes distribution, payments, ownership checks, refunds and store reviews under its own policies. O2 receives product pricing and purchase-entitlement information needed to unlock and restore access, not your card details. Device notification and speech services handle local reminders and voice guidance. A speech provider may use its own online service according to your device settings and its privacy policy; O2 supplies training cues, not your training history or health-screening selections.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy for their services.
