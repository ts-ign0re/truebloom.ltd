English

Peptide & Hormone Tracker

Keep a clear record of your prescribed therapy, one day at a time. Peptide & Hormone Tracker brings your schedule, dose history, weight and lab reports together on your iPhone or iPad.

YOUR EXISTING PLAN
Choose a therapy program and a suggested medicine name, or enter the name from your package. Record the form, amount and instructions from your prescription. Set daily, weekday, interval or cycle schedules, or keep a manual plan. The app does not select treatment or recommend a dose.

A PRACTICAL DAILY DIARY
• Record doses with saved amounts and timestamps.
• Keep past entries when your plan changes.
• Set local reminders with discreet notification text.
• Track weight and view its history; optionally import weight from Apple Health.
• Keep PDF lab reports and review text recognized on the device.
• Export your diary as JSON or delete app data in Settings.

ON YOUR DEVICE
No app account is required. Your diary is stored on this device, without iCloud sync or automatic backups. The developer has no server for your health records. Deleting the app or losing the device means losing the local diary, so export any records you need to keep.

OPTIONAL AI, YOUR CHOICE
Connect a supported AI provider using your own API key. The provider receives your messages, attached files and any diary or memory data you explicitly allow. New chats start with diary, plan and memory access off. Provider terms and charges apply; AI can make mistakes.

Use the app to document a plan agreed with your clinician. It does not diagnose, prescribe or replace medical care. Check medicine names, forms, amounts and schedules against your package and prescription.

By True Bloom Ltd, Cyprus. Support: info@truebloom.ltd.

Peptide & Hormone Tracker

Keep a clear record of your prescribed therapy, one day at a time. Peptide & Hormone Tracker brings your schedule, dose history, weight and lab reports together on your iPhone or iPad.

YOUR EXISTING PLAN
Choose a therapy programme and a suggested medicine name, or enter the name from your pack. Record the form, amount and instructions from your prescription. Set daily, weekday, interval or cycle schedules, or keep a manual plan. The app does not select treatment or recommend a dose.

A PRACTICAL DAILY DIARY
• Record doses with saved amounts and timestamps.
• Keep past entries when your plan changes.
• Set local reminders with discreet notification text.
• Track weight and view its history; optionally import weight from Apple Health.
• Keep PDF lab reports and review text recognised on the device.
• Export your diary as JSON or delete app data in Settings.

ON YOUR DEVICE
No app account is required. Your diary is stored on this device, without iCloud sync or automatic backups. The developer has no server for your health records. Deleting the app or losing the device means losing the local diary, so export any records you need to keep.

OPTIONAL AI, YOUR CHOICE
Connect a supported AI provider using your own API key. The provider receives your messages, attached files and any diary or memory data you explicitly allow. New chats start with diary, plan and memory access off. Provider terms and charges apply; AI can make mistakes.

Use the app to document a plan agreed with your clinician. It does not diagnose, prescribe or replace medical care. Check medicine names, forms, amounts and schedules against your pack and prescription.
