Explore job listings from sources around the world, including remote roles, seasonal work and listings tagged for visa sponsorship. Filter by location, field and experience, then keep a shortlist of the opportunities that fit.

Features

Global Job Feed

Browse listings refreshed throughout the day. Availability and details come from third-party sources; confirm them with the employer.

Smart Filters

Filter by remote, hybrid, on-site, category, experience level, and region.

ATS Optimizer

Use AI to tailor your CV to a selected job or a description you paste. Review the result, copy it or export a PDF. AI may make mistakes and cannot guarantee screening or hiring outcomes.

Favorites

Keep jobs and their saved, optimised CVs together on your device so you can return to an application later.

Push Notifications

Create alerts by country, field and keyword, and enable notifications for matching listings. Manage alert settings in the app.
