A different way to discover

Turn the dial. Follow the sound.

Start with rock in Ireland, jazz in Japan, or traditional music in Brazil. Choose a genre and tune to another country. Countries are a starting point for exploration: Apple Music results can include music from elsewhere and vary by catalog and storefront.

42 starting points

Explore countries and regions across the Americas, Europe, Africa, Asia and the Pacific.

Seven genres

Local, Rock, Pop, Jazz, Electronic, Soul and Traditional, with country-specific traditional search sets.

Roam and shuffle

Keep your genre and explore another country, try a random genre, and shuffle songs in playlists.

Full receiver, mini player

A receiver for your desk and a compact player in the menu bar. Hide the window and keep listening.

Save the songs you find

Add a playing song to your Apple Music library or send a Favorite request directly to Apple Music.

Made for your Mac

Tune with the dial, trackpad, scale or arrow keys. Six interface languages with eight regional versions and VoiceOver labels.

Support

Need a hand?

Email info@truebloom.ltd. Include your macOS version, World Radio version, selected country and genre, and the error text. Do not send your Apple Account password or other credentials.

Getting connected

Sign in to the Music app with an active Apple Music subscription. Open World Radio, choose Connect Apple Music and allow access. You can manage that permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Media & Apple Music.

No results or no playback?

Try another genre or country, or choose a different playlist or station from Music. Availability depends on your Apple Music storefront. Outputs with their own hardware volume control may disable the app’s output-volume control.

Changing the interface language

World Radio follows macOS language preferences. You can choose a per-app language in System Settings → General → Language & Region → Applications, then restart the player.

Your music, your account

No extra account. No recording.

World Radio uses Apple’s MusicKit for catalog search, playback and the library/favorites actions you choose. It has no advertising, analytics SDKs, developer-operated backend or audio recording. Apple processes Apple Music activity under its own privacy terms.
