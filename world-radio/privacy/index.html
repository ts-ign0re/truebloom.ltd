Privacy Policy

World Radio · Last updated November 3, 2026

World Radio is a native macOS music-discovery app provided by TrueBloom Ltd. It connects to Apple Music through Apple’s MusicKit. There is no separate World Radio account and no developer-operated service receiving your listening activity.

Information collected by the app developer

World Radio does not send us your identity, listening history, library, favorites, device identifiers or usage analytics. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising or tracking.

Apple Music and network requests

When you authorize Apple Music access, Apple’s frameworks manage the authorization credentials. The app sends catalog searches and playback requests to Apple. If you choose Add to Library or Favorite, it sends the selected song identifier and that action to Apple Music for your account. Artwork, when displayed, is obtained from Apple-provided URLs.

Apple processes the requests, account information and listening activity needed to provide Apple Music under its own terms and privacy policy. An Apple Music subscription and internet connection are required for playback. World Radio does not give us access to your Apple Account password or payment details.

Local app state

The app keeps the active country, genre, queue references and session confirmations for library/favorite actions in memory while running. One local preference remembers that you completed the welcome and Apple Music connection; it is not sent to us. Apple’s frameworks and macOS may retain their own authorization, playback and cache data. Library additions and favorites are stored in your Apple Music account by Apple; quitting or deleting World Radio does not remove them.

No audio recording

World Radio does not request microphone or system-audio capture permission and does not record audio. The output-volume control changes the Mac’s playback volume; it does not capture sound.

Control and deletion

You can revoke World Radio’s Apple Music permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Media & Apple Music. Manage saved songs, favorites, listening activity and account deletion through Apple Music and your Apple Account. There is no separate World Radio account or listening-history database for us to delete.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content you choose to send, and use them to handle your request. Please avoid sending passwords or sensitive account data. We keep support correspondence only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. For access, correction or deletion requests concerning correspondence held by us, contact info@truebloom.ltd.

This website

This policy describes the World Radio app. Its support pages are hosted as a static website. Hosting providers may process ordinary web request logs, and the shared site stylesheet loads fonts from Google Fonts. The app itself does not embed these pages or those website resources.

Changes and contact

Updates will be published here with a revised date. For privacy or support questions, email info@truebloom.ltd.
